Privacy Policy

Live Cricket Auction — last updated August 2026

This app runs a live, room-based cricket auction. This page explains what information it collects, why, and how it's stored. If anything here is unclear, contact the person who deployed/operates this instance of the app (the "operator") — see the contact note at the bottom.

What we collect

If you create a player account (to register yourself for auctions): a username, a password (never stored in plain text — it's hashed with scrypt before saving), your name, age, playing role, and an optional profile photo you upload.

If you create or join an auction room: the room name, team names, and auction settings you choose (purse, tiers, squad size). Rooms are protected by short PIN codes rather than personal logins — a Host PIN, one PIN per team, and a Player PIN, all generated randomly.

While an auction runs: bid amounts, which team bid what and when, and the resulting sale price/team for each player — this is the auction's own record, visible to everyone in that room.

We do not collect payment information, precise location, contacts, or device identifiers, and this app does not use advertising or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs.

How it's stored

Everything above is stored in a single database file on the server this instance is deployed to. Player photos are stored as part of that same database (not uploaded to a separate third-party image host). There is no data sharing with outside companies.

How it's used

Account details exist solely so a player's profile carries over between auctions instead of being re-entered each time, and so they can log back in on a different device. Room PINs exist solely to control who can view or act in a given auction room. None of this information is used for advertising, profiling, or sold to anyone.

Who can see what

  • Anyone with a room's Room ID can see that room's live auction (teams, players, bids) in read-only spectator mode — this is by design, the same way an in-person auction is visible to everyone in the room.
  • Your player account's saved profile (username, password) is never shown to other players — only the name/age/role/photo you choose to bring into a room is visible there.
  • The person running a room (the "host") can see and manage everything happening inside that specific room, including approving or rejecting player registrations.

Your choices

  • A profile photo is optional — you can register without one.
  • You can change your role/age just for a specific room without changing your saved account profile.
  • To remove your account or data, contact the operator of the specific instance you signed up on (see below) — this app does not currently have a self-service delete-account flow.

Children

This app is intended for general audiences running or joining casual sports auctions (e.g. a friends' league) and is not directed at children under 13. Account age fields are self-reported and used only for auction context (e.g. junior categories), not for age verification.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

This page describes the app's data handling in general. For questions about a specific deployed instance (e.g. what happens to your data on a particular Room ID), contact whoever shared that Room ID/link with you, as they are operating that instance.
